4. WHY THE UNMODIFIED FULL VALIDATOR DID NOT PASS

All three comparison programs call psutil.Process().memory_info() to collect optional memory telemetry. This sandbox cannot expose the running process's own PID to psutil, which raises NoSuchProcess. The programs therefore terminate before printing their JSON result. This is an execution-environment limitation at the telemetry step, not evidence that reward accounting or the prespecified fault checks failed.

The outer tools/validate_review_package.py then raises AttributeError when key() calls .items() on a crashed row's checks=None. It exits without producing its final validation.json. The unmodified failure log and raw comparison logs are retained. No blanket pass is claimed.

A separate diagnostic copy changes only peak_rss() in the three comparison programs: unavailable memory yields JSON null on NoSuchProcess/AccessDenied. No memory measurement is claimed, and this is explicitly NOT an unmodified reproduction of the hashed programs. Checks, fixtures, policies and numerical thresholds are unchanged. The three-file diff is supplied.
