Dear Editor,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "LunarLab: Auditable reward-design experiments in reinforcement
learning", for publication in Neurocomputing as a Software publication.

LunarLab is an MIT-licensed research toolkit for reward-design experiments. It records original and training
rewards separately, selects checkpoints on validation seeds, protects the selected policy for final testing,
and replays exported artifacts so that file integrity, numerical agreement and behavioral conclusions are
reported separately. The manuscript illustrates it with a LunarLander study in which a deliberately flawed
reward produces no strict landings, a prespecified fault-injection comparison with a separately implemented
Stable-Baselines3 reference, and a CartPole study through the same adapter-based layer. It also reports the
limits of this evidence, including a Linux replay of one policy that does not reproduce its recorded
aggregate.

The source code is public at https://github.com/SHAYAN-ABRAR/lunarlab and will remain accessible throughout
review; the manuscript cites the revisions it describes and lists the materials that are not yet public.
The use of AI tools in the software, the experiments and the manuscript is described in Section 5.3 and in
the AI declaration before the references.
